About DRD

Direction Towards Responsible Development is an advisory practice built around one fundamental idea: development must be responsible to be sustainable.

DRD was created to help organisations navigate the increasingly complex intersection of business, people, human rights, sustainability and governance. Over time, our work evolved from conventional consulting engagements into a more specialised strategic advisory practice. Today, DRD brings together deep field experience and strategic insight to help organisations address responsible-business challenges with greater clarity and practical action.

Our Approach

From Understanding to Action. Our approach connects global standards with practical implementation through a structured methodology:

01 — Understand

Understand the organisation, its operating context, stakeholders and value-chain realities.

02 — Identify

Identify material risks, gaps, vulnerabilities and opportunities.

03 — Prioritise

Focus resources on the issues that matter most to people, business resilience and responsible growth.

04 — Design

Develop practical systems, governance structures, processes and interventions.

05 — Implement

Translate recommendations into operational action.

06 — Measure

Track effectiveness, outcomes and areas requiring improvement.

07 — Strengthen

Build organisational capability so responsible practices become embedded rather than dependent on external intervention.

Standards Provide Direction

Our work draws on internationally recognised responsible-business principles, including the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, ILO standards, OECD guidance, IFC Performance Standards, and responsible procurement frameworks. We do not treat frameworks as checklists; we use them as tools for better decision-making.

What We Do

Responsible Business Advisory: Helping organisations understand risk, strengthen governance and create responsible systems across their operations and value chains.

1. ESG & Sustainability Governance

We help organisations strengthen governance structures through which ESG and sustainability considerations become part of business decision-making. Capabilities include ESG governance frameworks, materiality and risk perspectives, leadership accountability, policy development, and process integration.

2. Human Rights Due Diligence

Supporting organisations to identify, assess, prevent, mitigate and address human-rights risks. Capabilities cover human rights risk/impact assessments, worker rights protection, vulnerable worker groups, ethical recruitment, modern slavery risks, and supplier human-rights governance.

3. Responsible Supply Chains

Helping organisations strengthen supplier accountability, social sustainability and responsible sourcing practices. Capabilities include supplier responsibility frameworks, social compliance systems, capacity building, correct action plans, and worker engagement.

4. Grievance & Remediation

Designing and operationalising effective grievance mechanisms that provide people with a credible pathway to raise concerns and access remedy. Capabilities include system design, reporting channels, case management, investigation processes, and escalation frameworks.

5. Responsible Procurement

Embedding responsible-business considerations into procurement and supplier-management workflows. Capabilities include responsible procurement frameworks, supplier selection criteria, due diligence, policies, and performance tracking.

6. Board & Leadership Advisory

Providing strategic perspectives to boards, senior leadership and governance functions on emerging ESG and human-rights expectations.
